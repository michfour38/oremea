# Resonance seed audit — The Becoming

Internal working record for cross-room comparison. This file does not drive participant-facing content.

## Room boundary

- Room: The Becoming
- Theme: Embodiment, repetition, and lived continuation
- Seed job: collect the translation from understanding into repeated observable practice, including environment, low-capacity versions, keepable agreements, return after lapses and review; do not turn follow-through into identity or moral worth
- Voice: plain, human, concrete, first-read clear
- Movement: make it visible → repeat it → shape the environment → keep a small version alive → make keepable agreements → return quickly → continue with review
- Answer tolerance: short answers, uncertainty, `nothing`, `both`, `depends`, humour, contradiction, correction, and rejection of the premise all remain usable data
- Interpretation boundary: A missed practice is evidence of a missed practice, not laziness, failure, self-betrayal, lack of discipline, or identity. The practice must remain adjustable to real capacity and current conditions.

## Boundaries with earlier rooms

- **The Hearth** — conditions around belonging, welcome, fit, and relational participation.
- **The Mirror** — the participant's recurring move inside interaction and what tends to happen next.
- **The Garden** — care, support, labour, capacity, resources, receiving, asking, and sustainability.
- **The Bearing** — priority, trade-off, decision ownership, words/actions, and chosen direction.
- **The Pulse** — attraction, interest, desire, reality-testing, and chosen movement.
- **The Shadow** — strong reactions under pressure, familiar responses, past/present comparison, and updated choice.
- **The Forge** — conflict events, responsibility, repair, evidence, and relational distance.
- **The Vision** — concrete future shared-life design, agreements, resources, and prototypes.
- **The Gathering** — integration that tests connection without forcing unity and remains independent of prior rooms.

## Seven-day data arc

| Day | Main data being collected | Cross-section dimensions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Turn understanding into action | action-needed insight, visible behaviour, ordinary-day location, evidence, measurable action |
| 2 | Repeat something small | repeatable action, realistic frequency, cue, repetition benefit, practice design |
| 3 | Shape the environment | existing support, friction, environment edit, continuity support, immediate environment change |
| 4 | Low-capacity continuity | low-energy effect, minimum version, rest/drift distinction, bad-day continuity, low-capacity plan |
| 5 | Keepable agreements | kept promise, enabling conditions, unreliable follow-through, smaller agreement, seven-day agreement |
| 6 | Return after a lapse | lapse cue, quick restart, repair/resume distinction, one-day framing, return protocol |
| 7 | Continuation | new ability, chosen practice, lived measure, next learning edge, review plan |

## Cross-room overlap notes

- **Bearing / Becoming:** both look at words and actions. Bearing asks whether behaviour matches what the participant says matters; Becoming asks how one chosen understanding becomes a repeatable practice.
- **Vision / Becoming:** Vision designs shared systems; Becoming designs personal continuity around one practice. Environment and routine appear here only as support for repeated action.
- **Garden / Becoming:** capacity appears in both. Garden maps care and resource circulation; Becoming creates a deliberately smaller practice for low-capacity days.
- **Gathering / Becoming:** Gathering sorts and integrates meaning; Becoming takes one chosen piece into observable continuation.
- Distinct Becoming evidence: visible action, realistic frequency, environmental friction/support, minimum viable practice, keepable agreement, repair-versus-resume distinction, review date.

# Resonance seed audit — The Forge

Internal working record for cross-room comparison. This file does not drive participant-facing content.

## Room boundary

- Room: The Forge
- Theme: Relational pressure, conflict, and repair
- Seed job: collect what happens in conflict, what each person actually does or reports, what belongs to whom, what repair would look like, and what evidence changes participation; do not manufacture the other person's inner state
- Voice: plain, human, concrete, first-read clear
- Movement: notice tension → preserve listening → separate event, meaning and consequence → own a part → request repair → watch evidence → choose earlier participation
- Answer tolerance: short answers, uncertainty, `nothing`, `both`, `depends`, humour, contradiction, correction, and rejection of the premise all remain usable data
- Interpretation boundary: Do not invent the other person's perspective, impact, intention, remorse, or motive. Their experience enters as participant-reported words/actions, not model speculation. Repair is observable changed participation, not forced reconciliation.

## Boundaries with earlier rooms

- **The Hearth** — conditions around belonging, welcome, fit, and relational participation.
- **The Mirror** — the participant's recurring move inside interaction and what tends to happen next.
- **The Garden** — care, support, labour, capacity, resources, receiving, asking, and sustainability.
- **The Bearing** — priority, trade-off, decision ownership, words/actions, and chosen direction.
- **The Pulse** — attraction, interest, desire, reality-testing, and chosen movement.
- **The Shadow** — strong reactions under pressure, familiar responses, past/present comparison, and updated choice.

## Seven-day data arc

| Day | Main data being collected | Cross-section dimensions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Tension enters | observable shift, first response, side-of-story information, workability conditions, opening-minute sequence |
| 2 | Listening under pressure | listening change, closing cues, checking understanding, differing accounts, known/unknown evidence |
| 3 | What happened and what followed | specific event, consequence, misunderstanding/repair distinction, acknowledgement, responsibility sequence |
| 4 | Owning a part | own contribution, other contribution, responsibility boundary, changed action, responsibility map |
| 5 | Repair request | requested change, observable repair, consistency, choice after refusal, direct repair request |
| 6 | Repair evidence and distance | repair signs, continuation signs, participation threshold, available boundary, review threshold |
| 7 | Integration | conflict style, owned responsibility, repair skill, earlier action, participant-authored conflict plan |

## Cross-room overlap notes

- **Mirror / Forge:** both can map sequences. Mirror studies repeating interaction patterns generally; Forge studies conflict specifically and follows the sequence into responsibility and repair.
- **Shadow / Forge:** Shadow keeps the lens on the participant's strong reaction; Forge keeps the lens on the relational exchange, what each person did or reported, and what repair requires.
- **Garden / Forge:** requests appear in both. Garden asks for practical support; Forge asks for changed participation after a conflict or rupture.
- Distinct Forge evidence: conflict opening minute, known-versus-unknown account, event/consequence/intention/responsibility separation, observable repair, review threshold, distance when repair is unavailable.

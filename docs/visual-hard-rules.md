# Oremea visual hard rules

These are product-wide hard rules. They are not suggestions and should not be reinterpreted during UI cleanup.

1. **Black and neutral surfaces**
   - Use black, zinc, charcoal and neutral grey surfaces.
   - Do not introduce brown-tinted panels or yellow-washed surfaces.

2. **Headings are white / off-white**
   - Primary headings remain white or soft neutral white.

3. **Body copy is neutral**
   - Guidance, support text, previous answers, explanatory copy and placeholders use neutral grey / off-white.
   - Body copy must never be gold, yellow, amber, beige or brown-gold.

4. **One gold only**
   - The approved Oremea gold is `#C8A96A`.
   - Do not introduce alternate pale yellows, amber golds, beige golds or brown-golds.

5. **Gold is an accent, not a reading colour**
   - Gold may be used for small labels, progress, focus states, active borders and primary controls.
   - Gold must not be used for paragraphs or long-form reading text.

6. **Inputs must be obvious without glowing white**
   - Input surfaces stay dark.
   - Resting borders are neutral and clearly visible.
   - Focus may use `#C8A96A`.
   - Placeholder text must be readable and neutral.

7. **Consistency across products**
   - Recognition, Resonance and Compass follow the same palette discipline unless an explicit product-specific exception is approved.

8. **Do not revive retired warm variants**
   - Retired examples include `#D6B97A`, `#C6A96B`, `#C9B98F`, `#B6A477`, `#F1DFB4` and similar yellow/beige/amber variants.

When changing product UI, preserve these rules before making aesthetic interpretations.

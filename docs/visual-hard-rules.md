# Oremea visual hard rules

These are hard rules. Product palettes must not be normalised into one global palette during cleanup.

## Shared rule

- Preserve each product's approved visual identity.
- Functional changes must not silently restyle a product.
- Do not replace established colours because another Oremea product uses a different gold, border or reading colour.
- Any deliberate palette change requires explicit approval first.

## Recognition — frozen original palette

Recognition must retain the palette it used immediately before the 8 August 2026 visual cleanup (`ad19d633`). This is the approved Recognition baseline.

- Page background: `#0A0A0A`
- Main card surface: `#11100D`
- Raised surface: `#14110B`
- Main border: `#2A2418`
- Soft/disabled border: `#3A2F1C`
- Primary text/headings: `#EAEAEA`
- General body copy: `#BFBFBF`
- User/reflection answer copy: `#D8D0C0`
- Muted warm guidance / previous-question copy: `#8F815E`
- Recognition gold: `#C6A96B`
- Bright gold hover/emphasis: `#D6B97A`
- Textarea placeholder: `#666`
- Input placeholder: `#777`

Recognition-specific rules:

- The muted warm guidance colour `#8F815E` is intentional. Keep it muted; do not brighten it into yellow or pale gold.
- Question headings remain light/white.
- Textareas remain almost black with the subtle `#2A2418` resting border.
- Focus may use `#C6A96B` without adding a bright glow.
- Active primary controls may use `#C6A96B` with `#D6B97A` for hover/emphasis.
- Do not substitute `#C8A96A`, `#C9B98F`, `#B6A477`, `#B59A60`, or other later cleanup colours into Recognition unless explicitly approved.

## Resonance

Resonance keeps its separately approved single-gold system using `#C8A96A`. Do not use the Resonance palette as a reason to restyle Recognition.

## Compass and other products

Preserve their existing approved palette until a product-specific palette is explicitly recorded here.

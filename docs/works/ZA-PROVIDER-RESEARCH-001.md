## Data-model findings from batch 001

The first live provider batch exposed two useful gaps and both were corrected:

1. A provider can have multiple physical locations inside one market. `works_provider_locations` now hangs below `works_provider_markets`.
2. Lead time needs a basis. `works_offerings.lead_time_basis` now distinguishes `WORKING_DAYS` from `CALENDAR_DAYS`.

The research also expanded the capability vocabulary with `CRUSHING`, `CHEMICAL_TESTING`, `PHYSICAL_TESTING`, and `SENSORY_TESTING` without requiring a schema migration.

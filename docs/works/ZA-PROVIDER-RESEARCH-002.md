# WORKS ZA provider research — batch 002

Research date: 2026-07-29

## Catercorp

Website: https://www.catercorp.co.za/

Official pages reviewed:

- https://www.catercorp.co.za/about-us/
- https://www.catercorp.co.za/what-we-do/
- https://www.catercorp.co.za/research-and-development/

Confirmed from provider-controlled public sources:

- South African manufacturer of sauces, marinades, condiments and related food products.
- Johannesburg facility at 757 6th Street, Wynberg, Gauteng.
- Private-label manufacturing and custom recipe/product development.
- In-house food technologists and R&D kitchen.
- Cooking/manufacturing capability for sauce products.
- Private-label bottling.
- Bottling into glass or plastic bottle designs is described.
- Labelling and packaging support are described.
- Provider publishes a minimum cooking quantity of 375 kg and cooking-pot capacities from roughly 375 litres upward.
- Provider-controlled pages state that its product offerings/factory are Halaal and Kosher certified and that its development process can facilitate Halaal/Kosher certification.

Trust treatment:

The provider's current Halaal statements are retained as research context, but the WORKS matching fixture does not yet treat the required Halaal credential as authority-verified. Current certification scope and validity should be checked against the relevant certification authority before that hard requirement becomes a confirmed match.

Current unknowns:

- How a 500-bottle target maps to Catercorp's published minimum cooking quantity without knowing bottle volume/product density.
- Current available production capacity.
- Current production lead time for a new custom sauce.
- Current authoritative Halaal certificate scope/expiry.

Why this provider was added:

The first provider batch was weighted toward dry-food manufacturing. Catercorp provides a real liquid-sauce manufacturing/bottling route and gives the chilli-sauce fixture a relevant production candidate without weakening matching rules.

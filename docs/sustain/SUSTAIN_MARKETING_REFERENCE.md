# Sustain Marketing Reference

## Core promise
Sustain reduces household mental load by giving daily life a reliable place to be captured, remembered, organized, and returned at the moment it matters.

The user does not need to hold the household in working memory.

The more transparent and abundant the information, the more accurately Sustain can hold continuity and protect capacity.

## Primary CTA direction
**Let's be open and honest here.**

Tell Sustain what is actually happening.

The clearer and more complete the information, the more accurately Sustain can remember, organize, connect, and return what matters when it is useful.

## Psychological marketing position
Sustain is not primarily sold as budgeting software, a task manager, a receipt scanner, a meal planner, or a parenting app.

Those are expressions of one deeper function:

> A household no longer has to live entirely inside one person's working memory.

Marketing should lead with the felt experience:
- mental load being set down
- continuity being held
- details being remembered without vigilance
- household patterns becoming visible
- decisions becoming easier because context has already been retained
- progress becoming visible
- capacity returning to the people carrying the household

The emotional promise is relief through remembered continuity.

## Core marketing lines
- **Come home to a home that remembers.**
- **One thought at a time. One less thing to carry.**
- **Capture the thought. Keep the capacity.**
- **Set it down once. Find it when it matters.**
- **The thought arrives. Sustain remembers.**
- **Reduce mental load. Restore capacity.**

## Transparency principle
Sustain becomes more useful through participation.

It should communicate this openly rather than pretending it can infer a household perfectly from sparse data:

> **The more transparent and abundant the information, the more accurately Sustain can hold continuity and protect your capacity.**

This is both a product principle and a CTA.

It invites users to record ordinary life without requiring polished input.

## Capture-first behaviour
The product should make recording easier than remembering.

Capture can include:
- receipts
- bank statements
- purchases
- meals
- preferences
- chores
- birthdays and important dates
- household rules
- reminders
- repairs and replacement needs
- payments and allocations
- child requests
- household events
- passing thoughts
- temporary frustrations
- things to revisit later

The system then decides what should become durable memory, a reminder, a pattern, a preference, a financial record, or merely contextual history.

## Live household examples — 2026-08-08
These are reference examples of the kind of ordinary information Sustain should be able to receive without requiring the user to pre-classify it.

### Social / whereabouts
Megan and Hannah are at a market with Seth, Leah, and Cliffy.

### Household rule / child frustration
Logan is upset that unused phone time does not accumulate.

Existing household rule: **maximum 2 hours of phone time per day.**

The system should distinguish the standing rule from today's emotional response to it.

### Activity / food
Jamie is making orange juice from oranges.

This could later contribute to household food memory, preferred activities, ingredient use, or meal context without forcing those classifications at capture time.

## Product-design insight from live use
The creator is simultaneously living household life, recording it, and discovering the product through use.

Reference line:

> **I'm recording, and by doing so I'm designing the world's coolest parents' guide — one slip at a time.**

The important product insight is not the wording alone. Sustain should be capable of being designed from reality rather than requiring reality to fit a predetermined filing system.

## Marketing principle: do not sell surveillance or perfection
Sustain should never imply that a good household records everything perfectly.

The invitation is participation, not compliance.

A user can start with one receipt, one thought, one household rule, one meal, or one reminder.

Accuracy grows as participation grows.

## Trust principle
Sustain must distinguish:
- fact
- user-stated rule
- preference
- inference
- unresolved ambiguity
- reminder / future obligation
- historical event
- temporary emotional state

The system should not silently convert ambiguous input into certainty.

## Positioning against generic productivity tools
Generic productivity software asks the user to decide where information belongs before storing it.

Sustain should allow:

> **Tell me what is happening. I will help make it usable.**

The user supplies reality.
Sustain supplies continuity, organization, and retrieval.

## Reference concept
**Capture first. Organize continuously. Return context when it matters.**

This is the foundational behaviour underneath receipts, budgeting, meals, family preferences, chores, shopping intelligence, dates, reminders, and future household features.
